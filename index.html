About

I'm a multimedia designer with over a decade of experience across video editing, motion design and visual storytelling, in agency and in-house roles alongside freelance work.

Since 2021 I've focused on visual journalism, translating complex stories, data and reporting into clear, high-impact visual narratives across illustration, layout and design for editorial and humanitarian content. I lead the creative team at Beam Reports, where I've produced and directed more than 100 videos and designed the posters, infographics and campaigns behind the platform's reporting.

"Design has to work, art does not." Donald Judd

  1. Read the source

    I start with the report, investigation or story itself, to find what's worth spotlighting.

  2. Sketch for structure

    Thumbnail sketches test pacing and hierarchy before any illustration begins.

  3. Then design

    Visual direction comes next. Design is the last step, not the first.
